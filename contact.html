Before you ask

Quick answers

What happens after I submit an inquiry? +
A SENTINEL program lead reviews your inquiry, confirms compliance fit, and reaches out within two business days to schedule a closed brief and prepare a tailored proposal.
Do you support FMS (Foreign Military Sales)? +
Yes. Our allied procurement team works with DSCA and partner-nation procurement offices throughout the FMS process, and we also support direct commercial sales where an approved license is in place.
Can SENTINEL accommodate classified discussions? +
Yes. We hold facility clearance and can host classified discussions at our Arlington and Huntsville locations, or travel to your program office's secure environment.
Are demonstrations available? +
Yes. We run live capability demonstrations at our Fort Bliss test range monthly, and conduct user-jury evaluations with operating units worldwide on request.
Which contracting vehicles are available? +
SENTINEL is on GSA Schedule, SEWP V, and several IDIQ vehicles. We also support OTA agreements through xTechSearch, DIU, and AFWERX channels.
