About SENTINEL

Built for the operators who use it

Founded by former service members and roboticists, SENTINEL exists to deliver unmanned systems that actually survive contact with the field. We design every platform in-house — chassis, perception, autonomy, and sustainment — and we test alongside the units that fly them.

Mission

Keep people out of the most dangerous parts of the fight by giving them machines they can trust to go in first — for reconnaissance, logistics, security, and recovery.

Principles

Human-on-the-loop. Built to standard. Sustainable in the field. Every system we ship is operator-tested, allied-aligned, and compliant with U.S. and partner export controls.
