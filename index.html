About Intellectual Robotics

Built for the operators who use it

Founded by engineers and roboticists in Uzbekistan, Intellectual Robotics (iRobotics) delivers unmanned and IoT systems built for real-world field conditions. We design every platform in-house — chassis, perception, real-time telemetry, autonomy, and sustainment.

Mission

Keep people out of hazardous environments by giving them intelligent machines they can trust to go in first — for reconnaissance, IoT monitoring, logistics, security, and recovery.

Principles

Human-on-the-loop. Built to standard. Sustainable in the field. Every system we ship is operator-tested and engineered for reliable deployment.
