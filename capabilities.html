Perception

Full situational awareness, day or night

A fused sensor suite builds a live 3D model of the environment thirty times a second — accurate to the centimeter in rain, dust, and low light.

  • Multi-spectral EO/IR — visible, near-IR, and thermal

Autonomy

Navigates and plans without external signals

Visual-inertial odometry, terrain-relative navigation, and on-platform mission planning keep platforms operational when GNSS is jammed, spoofed, or unavailable.

  • GPS-denied navigation with sub-meter drift over 10 km
  • 275 TOPS of neural compute on board — fully disconnected operation
  • Terrain reasoning for slopes, ditches, and overhead clearance
  • Behavior trees designed and reviewed with end-users

Communications

Resilient links in contested spectrum

Anti-jam waveforms and self-healing mesh keep distributed fleets connected when adversary EW is active — without depending on commercial infrastructure.

  • Frequency-hopping mesh with adaptive bandwidth
  • Optional SATCOM backhaul for beyond-line-of-sight operation
  • End-to-end encrypted command and telemetry
  • Graceful degradation — return-to-base on link loss

Safety & Authority

Human-on-the-loop, every consequential action

  • Redundant emergency stops, hardware and software
  • Geo-fencing, no-go zones, and per-area speed limits
  • Full mission log retained for after-action review
