Good to know

Common questions

Do SENTINEL platforms operate autonomously without an operator? +
Platforms execute navigation, mapping, and sensor tasks autonomously, but every consequential action — kinetic effects, payload release, or interaction with humans — requires explicit operator authority. We design for human-on-the-loop, not human-out-of-the-loop.
Do the platforms work without GPS? +
Yes. Visual-inertial odometry and terrain-relative navigation maintain sub-meter accuracy over kilometers in GNSS-denied environments. GPS is used when available but never required.
How quickly can a unit be fielded? +
Crate to operational in under an hour with a two-person team. Initial operator training is a two-day course; sustainment training is built into the platform itself.
How does SENTINEL handle export controls? +
All SENTINEL platforms are export controlled under ITAR. International sales require an approved license. We work with partner-nation procurement offices and FMS channels throughout the process.
What sustainment is included? +
Every unit ships with five years of spares, training, and 24/7 engineering support. Mean repair time is under two hours at the field level using line-replaceable units.
